2D Visualization of Incompressible Flow Through Machine Learning

Thesis, 2024

Combines machine learning with traditional computational fluid dynamics to predict fluid flow in mechanical systems. It explores hybrid approaches that reduce computational cost and benchmarks them against conventional methods.

MHD Free Convection with Joule Heating and Entropy Generation inside an H-shaped Chamber

Journal paper, Heliyon, 2023

Numerical study of natural convection of water in an H-shaped chamber under a magnetic field and Joule heating, varying Rayleigh and Hartmann numbers. Heat transfer is assessed through Nusselt numbers, temperature, entropy generation and thermal criteria, with streamlines and isotherms showing the flow.

Air Conditioner Using Peltier Module

Project, 2023

An air conditioner built on a thermoelectric (Peltier) module and locally available parts. Covers module, fin, fan motor and pump selection, circuit design, SolidWorks modelling and an economic analysis for manufacturing.

Cooling Load Estimation

Project, 2023

Models a room in SolidWorks and estimates its cooling load from climate data, covering conduction, solar radiation, infiltration and internal gains. Ends with the refrigeration tonnage needed, documented in a LaTeX report.
